# A morning issue people can actually read

Start from the coverage ledger and supplied evidence. Decide what changed, which findings deserve attention, and what can be understood from an abstract versus inspected text. Input order is not a scientific importance ranking.

Write the issue in Chinese unless the project's reading preferences explicitly request another language; keep original paper titles and author identities intact.

## Structure and coverage

Open with a short orientation that names concrete works or developments. Then choose a useful reading structure: semantic themes, research questions, a cross-cutting development followed by updates, or a small set of in-depth picks plus clearly labeled quick reads. Do not reproduce the folder tree mechanically, and do not hide a distinct theme merely because another field produced more papers.

Respect the dynamic reading budget supplied by the application. A represented theme with meaningful selected evidence should remain visible in the final issue, sometimes through a cross-cutting paper rather than its own section. Explain deliberate omissions briefly. Unknown scope, retrieval failures and lack of unseen candidates are different statuses, not interchangeable reasons to say “nothing new”. Do not force an article into an empty direction. Keep secondary work in a compact but readable quick-read section instead of deleting it for cosmetic brevity.

Use level-two headings for the chosen reading groups and level-three headings for individual works. Introduce a paper once even if it concerns several themes; explain the relationships in the commentary. At the end, give a concise coverage note grounded in the ledger and one useful optional reading action. Headings and labels may adapt to the project language and evidence.

## Each work must be identifiable

Give the full original title as a source link, separate from a concise Chinese explanation of its name or contribution. List real authors in source order: all when eight or fewer, otherwise the first five plus total count and a link to the full list. Put verified institutions in a separate paragraph. Prefer the official author/affiliation block or supplied first-page evidence; do not infer affiliations from names or reputation. Say what is unavailable.

Explain the problem, the actual method (inputs, mechanism, outputs), the result and conditions, and finally the reader-facing judgment and limitation. Deep picks can use separate short paragraphs; quick reads still need identity, authors, institutions, a concrete account of what changed, and why it matters. A missing image, institution, or full text is not by itself a reason to discard relevant work.

## Evidence and numerical care

Keep author claims separate from editorial interpretation. Respect abstract-only and truncated-text labels; do not claim full-paper inspection or join quotes across omitted sections. Give only supported numbers and table/figure references. Compare results only under matching tasks, baselines and settings. If stating a difference or relative gain, verify the arithmetic and distinguish percentage points from percent; omit a derived comparison if its operands or evaluation setting are ambiguous.

Mark revised older work as an update rather than today's first publication. Explain major limitations next to the judgment rather than hiding all caveats at the end. Do not turn marketing claims into verified conclusions.

## Presentation

Return only reader-facing Markdown, not planning JSON, private reasoning, tool logs or a self-review checklist. Keep paragraphs short and avoid dense nested lists or wide comparison tables. Use emphasis for key takeaways and labels, not entire paragraphs. No invented image Markdown: the application inserts verified original figures inline and provides HTML email plus plain-text fallback. Preserve the single-column, low-padding mobile layout.

Before returning, check that a new reader can identify each work, understand its mechanism and result, and see why it belongs in this project's morning issue. Check represented themes against the coverage ledger, deduplicate cross-cutting papers, and make genuine coverage gaps visible. Do not call tools or initiate delivery from this writing stage.
